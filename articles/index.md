# Articles

### Workflows

- [Getting started with
  bwTools](https://renscq.github.io/bwTools/articles/getting-started.md):

  Read, inspect, retrieve, summarize, merge, and write signal tracks
  through the standard BwgTrack workflow.

- [Reading and writing signal
  tracks](https://renscq.github.io/bwTools/articles/reading-writing.md):

  Read BigWig, WIG, and bedGraph files and write standardized signal
  outputs and manifests.

- [Regional retrieval and
  statistics](https://renscq.github.io/bwTools/articles/retrieve-stats.md):

  Use indexed regional retrieval, chromosome-boundary rules, and exact
  or zoom-aware interval statistics.

- [Merging and multi-sample
  workflows](https://renscq.github.io/bwTools/articles/merge-multisample.md):

  Work with multiple samples and strands and choose conservative or
  arithmetic merge semantics.

- [File formats and coordinate
  conventions](https://renscq.github.io/bwTools/articles/file-formats.md):

  Understand format detection, coordinate conversion, chromosome names,
  sequence metadata, and zoom metadata.

- [Performance diagnostics and compatibility
  validation](https://renscq.github.io/bwTools/articles/performance-compatibility.md):

  Profile large BigWig workflows and validate optional external BigWig
  implementations.
